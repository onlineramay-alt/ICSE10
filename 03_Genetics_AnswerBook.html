Q24. Lamarck proposed the theory of 'Use and Disuse' of organs (Mendel proposed the Laws of Inheritance/laws of segregation and independent assortment).

Model Answer (not an official mark scheme)

Q25. (a) Cross: Mother is a carrier (XᶜX) × Father is normal (XY) → offspring: XᶜX (carrier daughter), XX (normal daughter), XᶜY (colour-blind son), XY (normal son). The colourblind child inherits the Xᶜ from its carrier mother and a Y from its normal father. (b) The colourblind child must be male (XᶜY), since a son needs only one defective X (from a carrier mother) to be colour blind, while a daughter would need the defective allele from both parents.

Model Answer (not an official mark scheme)

Q26. (a) 50% of their sons will be colour blind (sons get X from mother: half get Xᶜ → colour blind, half get X → normal; the Y always comes from the colour-blind father and doesn't affect sons' vision). (b) Yes — a daughter can be colour blind here, because the mother can pass on Xᶜ and the father (being colour blind, XᶜY) always passes on his Xᶜ, so a daughter can be XᶜXᶜ (colour blind).

Model Answer (not an official mark scheme)

Q27. Haemophilia is a sex-linked recessive disorder in which blood fails to clot normally due to a deficiency of clotting factor, causing excessive bleeding even from minor injuries. Colour blindness is a sex-linked recessive disorder affecting the cone cells of the retina, causing an inability to distinguish between certain colours (commonly red and green) — it does not affect blood clotting or bleeding.
