Q21. The burning furnace in a closed room emits carbon monoxide (CO). CO combines with haemoglobin far more readily than oxygen does, forming a very stable compound called carboxyhaemoglobin, which cuts off the supply of oxygen to body tissues — causing the workers to die of oxygen starvation (asphyxiation) in their sleep.
