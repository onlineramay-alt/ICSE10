Q42. Karyokinesis is the division of the nucleus and occurs first; it results in the formation of two daughter nuclei. Cytokinesis is the division of the cytoplasm, occurs after karyokinesis, and results in two separate daughter cells.

Model Answer (not an official mark scheme)

Q43. In an animal cell, cytokinesis occurs by the formation of a cleavage furrow that constricts the cell membrane inward from the periphery until the cell is pinched into two. In a plant cell, since a rigid cell wall prevents furrowing, cytokinesis occurs by the formation of a cell plate that grows outward from the centre of the cell to the periphery, dividing it into two.
