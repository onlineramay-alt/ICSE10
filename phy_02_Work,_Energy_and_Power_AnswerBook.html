Q54. Let a body of mass m be released from rest at a height h. At the start its potential energy is mgh and its kinetic energy is zero, so the total is mgh. After falling a distance x its height is (h − x), so U = mg(h − x). Using v² = 2gx, its kinetic energy is K = ½mv² = ½m(2gx) = mgx. The total is therefore mg(h − x) + mgx = mgh. Just before striking the ground x = h, so U = 0 and K = mgh. At every point the total is mgh, which verifies that U + K is constant.
