Q15. (a) Osmosis (b) Rises/increases (c) Water (d) Sugar (solute) (e) Water

Official Answer

Q16. Plasmolysis is the shrinkage of the protoplasm of a plant cell away from its cell wall due to exosmosis (loss of water), when the cell is placed in a hypertonic (concentrated) solution. Application: it is used in food preservation — salting of pickles/meat and adding sugar to make jams draws water out of microbial cells by plasmolysis, killing them and preventing spoilage.
