Q25. The growth of the pollen tube down through the style towards the ovary, guided by the chemicals (sugars and proteins/peptones) secreted by the ovule, is a classic example of chemotropism.

Q26. Tropic movements are directional -- the direction of movement is determined by the direction of the stimulus (e.g. shoots bending towards light). Nastic movements are non-directional -- the movement occurs regardless of the direction of the stimulus and instead depends on factors like differential turgor changes (e.g. closing of stomata, folding of leaflets in Mimosa on touch).

Official Answer
